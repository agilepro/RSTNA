# Upcoming Events



## General Meeting - Oct 15, 2026

The Bay Area Air District (BAAD) proposal to improve air quality would phase out natural gas water heaters and furnaces and require that new water heaters and furnaces be electric. The BAAD electrification proposal has strong supporters and opponents.  
 
The Rancho Santa Teresa Neighborhood Association (RSTNA) is hosting a meeting that includes speakers with different perspectives. They will provide you with the information you need to understand the proposal, the status of implementation, the on-the-ground experiences of residents, and the resources (including rebates) that are available to you. 
 
When: **Thursday, October 15, 7:00 pm**
 
Where: Rancho Santa Teresa Swim and Racquet Club 
286 Sorrento Way, San Jose, CA 95119
 
Also online - Zoom link: https://sanjoseca.zoom.us/j/99580188861
