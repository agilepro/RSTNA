## Bay Area Air District (BAAD) Electrification Proposal
John McKenna (10 minutes)
 
#### Bio –
John McKenna is the father of a 12-year-old who has committed to improving lives now and creating a better future for our children and future generations. He co-leads the Sierra Club Loma Prieta chapter's Climate Action Leadership, working to advance local climate action plans, adoption of building reach codes, and implementation of regional and statewide air-regulator appliance standards. He serves on Menlo Park's Environmental Quality Commission and is a former chair. On many weekends, you will find him volunteering with Sunwork.org to help install heat pump water heaters and rooftop solar.  He is a member of the Bay Area Clean Air (BACA) Coalition.
 
#### Summary of presentation – 
John works on advancing the Bay Area Air District's zero NOx appliance rules such that they can be successfully implemented economically and equitably.  He collaborates with other organizations such as RMI, 350 Silicon Valley, Menlo Spark, Sierra Club, Acterra and many others.

## Challenges Experienced with the BAAD Proposal
Michael A Sibilia (10 minutes)
 
#### Bio -
Mike has forty-six years of experience in real estate beginning with Bishop Hawk Real Estate/Wayne Mascia Commercial Real Estate (1980-1991) and currently with Coldwell Banker Real Estate, Saratoga, CA (1991-2001).  He is the Broker/Owner and Founder of Keller Williams Realty Silicon Valley (2002-Present) with 150 sales associates.  He has many awards and recognition to his credit, including President of Santa Clara County Association of Realtors (2011), Realtor of the Year for Santa Clara County (2012), National Association of Realtors Board of Director (2015-2020), California Association of Realtors Board of Director (2005-Present), and he was named Director for Life for California Association of Realtors (2026).
 
Besides being the Broker of Record for the office, Mike represents buyers and sellers in Santa Clara County and South San Mateo County. Per the Santa Clara County Association of Realtors, he has consistently been ranked in the top 10% of member Realtors in production each year.
 
#### Summary of presentation –
Mike will provide awareness about the gas ban mandate and it’s ramifications.  Back in March of 2023, the Bay Area Air District (BAAD) voted to phase out new natural gas water heaters and furnaces with electric replacements across all nine Bay Area counties.  Many homeowners will experience huge expenses and delays based on the age and condition of their home that they cannot afford. Costs are not the water heater, or furnace but in many cases the electrical infrastructure required to operate them. 
 
Materials – May provide
 
 
## Information and Experience with Consumers
James Tuleya (10 minutes)
 
#### Bio -
James is the Community Programs Director and the Senior Energy Coach for Home Energy Analytics, where he works with communities and households through PG&E's free HomeIntel energy analysis and savings program to help residents understand and reduce their home energy use, along with planning for home electrification upgrades.
 
Previously, he spent eight years working on energy-efficiency programs and regulatory issues at PG&E, following an earlier career in technology, consulting, and service as a U.S. Navy officer.  He graduated from Stanford University and also has an MBA from the UCLA Anderson School of Business.
 
#### Summary of presentation - 
James will provide practical advice for how residents can use their own energy meter data and free analyses customized to their own homes to plan for future home electrification upgrades, while also identifying ways to reduce their current energy bills. He will share findings from tens of thousands of others previously helped in order to inform what one's electric panel capacity can actually handle, and things to keep in mind for how to best plan for future home heat pump upgrades for water heating and home heating. He will provide this in the context of information about the free HomeIntel program analysis that helps residents understand all their current gas and electricity usage and the program's free energy coaching to help them plan to electrify and save.
 
Materials –
James will bring flyers about the HomeIntel program.
